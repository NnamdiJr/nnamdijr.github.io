Data science, in context.

I’m Nnamdi Offor, a senior data scientist based in the San Francisco Bay Area. My specialty is cloud infrastructure: applying analytical thinking to the complex systems that power digital products.

My experience spans statistical analysis, machine learning, experimentation, and data engineering. I’m interested in the full path from an ambiguous question to a well-supported decision, not just the model in the middle.

Looking toward new frontiers.

Space and astronomy have long captured my imagination. They’re a reminder of what careful observation and ambitious engineering can reveal. Robotics is a growing interest: a place where learning systems meet the physical world.

These are directions I’m exploring, grounded in the same curiosity that drew me to data science.

A wider perspective.

I lived in Beijing from 2010 to 2014, and languages and different ways of seeing the world remain important to me. Outside work, I’m a husband, a father, and an enthusiastic learner.
